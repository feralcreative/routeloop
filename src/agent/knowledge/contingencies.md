# Contingencies: what to do when the plan breaks

Use this when a rider asks "what if", when a route crosses remote country, and when you finish a plan for a trip of more than a day. A good plan has a way out of each stretch that could go wrong; say where they are, briefly, rather than listing every disaster.

## For each long or remote route, know

- **The bailout.** The nearest road off the route that reaches a town with fuel, food, and a bed, and roughly where it leaves the route. On a mountain or desert route, name it.
- **The next fuel after the planned one.** If a planned station is closed (small-town stations close early, some only take cash, card readers fail), where is the next, and can the shortest tank reach it? get_fuel_plan and find_along_route answer this.
- **The no-signal stretches.** Phones lose signal in mountains, deserts, and national forests. Keep the ride on every phone (Keep on the ride page) and carry the route in the GPS before them. Agree on what to do if the group splits there: stop at the next named place and wait.
- **Help.** On a remote route, the nearest town with a motorcycle shop or dealer and a tow that will carry a bike. Many tow companies cannot; say so and suggest the rider check their roadside plan's motorcycle coverage before the trip.

## Weather and roads

- **A weather fallback for any day over a high pass or through a desert in summer**: a lower or shorter alternate (Routeloop alternates are made for this), or a rest day to wait it out.
- **Thunderstorms build over high country in the afternoon in summer**: plan passes for the morning.
- **A closure found the day before**: get_road_reports and the official source (fresh-intel), then an alternate route or a changed day.

## Time

- **Slack.** On a trip of more than three days, one easy or rest day (or a short day) absorbs a breakdown, a weather day, or a tired group. A plan with every day at the limit has no room for anything.
- **Late arrivals.** If a day runs long, the place to stop early is a town before dark, not a stretch of road. Name one on any long day.

## People

- **A rider who cannot continue** (unwell, bike down): the group decides in advance whether everyone waits or the group splits. Routeloop can split a group off at a stop and give them their own route home.
- **Contacts and papers** for everyone, traded before the trip (see trip-prep).

Keep it short in an answer: two or three contingencies that fit this ride, not the whole list.
