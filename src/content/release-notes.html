What's new

Routeloop is in a closed alpha and changes most days. The newest build is at the top, and the version in the page footer tells you which one you are on.

2026-08-23 23 August 2026

  • Every point you drop now starts as a point of interest. Drop pins wherever you are thinking of going, in whatever order, then promote the ones the route actually has to run through — "Make this a stop" in the row's ⋮ menu. The road is drawn once two of them are stops. The first point of each day is promoted for you.
  • Changed your mind? "Make this a POI" puts it back, and the point keeps its notes, categories and reservations.
  • Dragging a row reorders it, whichever kind it is. Dragging a point of interest no longer moves its pin.
  • This page, and a version number in the footer, so you can tell which build you found something in.

2026-08-22 22 August 2026

  • Buttons look like highway signs. The green field and white keyline are the house style now rather than something a few buttons opted into.
  • Fixed: you could not click into the search box at the bottom of a day. It could be tabbed to but not clicked.
  • Each day has its own + Point button. Press it, then click the map, and the point lands on that day.
  • The + Day button looked broken. It always worked — it just had no styling at all.

2026-08-21 21 August 2026

  • Saved places. Keep a library of the places you use — home, the good fuel stop, the meet point everyone knows — and drop one into any ride from the day's search box. File them into groups if you want to.
  • Stop details. A stop can hold a confirmation number, check-in and check-out, a phone number, an address, links and notes. Only you can see them. They never go out with a shared link and they are stripped from every export except your own backup file.
  • A place you save carries its phone number and address into every ride you use it in, but never a confirmation number — that belongs to one trip.

Found something wrong? The red button on the map is the fastest way to tell us, and it brings the details of what you were doing with it.
